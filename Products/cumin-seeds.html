Cumin Seeds

Unjha Sourcing

Cumin from Asia's Largest
Seed Trading Market

Unjha APMC in North Gujarat is Asia's single largest cumin trading market — and it's where RPM Global Exports builds its supplier base for cumin seeds. By visiting Unjha directly and maintaining relationships with established commission agents and farmers, we're able to source cumin that meets international grade requirements at prices that reflect actual market conditions.

We offer three primary grades: Singapore Quality (99.5% purity, preferred by Asian importers), Europe Quality (99% purity, suitable for EU food regulations), and FAQ for general trade. Each shipment is accompanied by a detailed spec sheet. Packaging options include 25 kg PP bags or jute bags per buyer preference.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions from Importers

What is the minimum order quantity for cumin seeds?
Our minimum order quantity for cumin seeds is 1 MT per shipment. Trial orders below 1 MT are considered case by case — contact us to discuss your requirement.
Can we request a sample before placing a bulk order?
Yes, we provide pre-shipment samples for quality verification. Contact us at meet@rpmglobalexports.com to request one.
What certifications does RPM Global Exports hold?
We are registered with IEC and APEDA, ensuring compliance with Indian export regulations and international quality standards.
What are your payment terms?
We work with T/T (Telegraphic Transfer). Terms are discussed based on order size and buyer profile.
How long does shipment take to the USA or Germany?
Typical transit time is 18–25 days to the USA and 15–20 days to Germany via sea freight.
Do you provide a Certificate of Analysis with each shipment?
Yes, every shipment includes a full COA covering purity, moisture content, and microbial standards.
