Cumin Seeds

Unjha Sourcing

Cumin from Asia's Largest
Seed Trading Market

Unjha APMC in North Gujarat is Asia's single largest cumin trading market — and it's where RPM Global Exports builds its supplier base for cumin seeds. By visiting Unjha directly and maintaining relationships with established commission agents and farmers, we're able to source cumin that meets international grade requirements at prices that reflect actual market conditions.

We offer three primary grades: Singapore Quality (98–99% purity, machine cleaned — reliable standard export grade for Middle East and Africa), Europe Quality (99.5%+ purity, sortex cleaned, EU pesticide MRL compliant — for regulated markets and premium retail), and FAQ for general trade, oil extraction, and price-sensitive bulk buyers. Each shipment comes with a detailed spec sheet.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions from Importers

What are the typical oil content and moisture specs for cumin seeds?
Volatile oil content: 2.5–3.5% (varies by grade and season). Moisture: below 10%. Full spec sheets for Singapore, Europe, and FAQ grades are available on request.
What foreign matter and cleanliness standard does each grade meet?
Europe Quality (99.5%+ purity): sortex cleaned, foreign matter below 0.5%. Singapore Quality (98–99%): machine cleaned. FAQ grade: cleaned, for oil extraction and blending. Spec sheet provided with every shipment.
What is the MOQ, and can I trial before committing to bulk?
MOQ is 1 MT. First-time buyers can trial from 500 kg. A free pre-shipment sample is available before you commit — request one at info@rpmglobalexports.com.
What certifications cover your cumin seed exports?
We hold IEC (DFLPJ8868G) and GST (24DFLPJ8868G1ZW). Spice Board registration — the statutory authority for cumin exports — is in progress and being finalised.
What packaging is standard for cumin seeds?
Standard is 25 kg PP woven bags. 50 kg bags and custom labelling available on request. We send packing and loading photos via WhatsApp before every shipment.
Do you provide a Certificate of Analysis with each shipment?
Yes, every shipment includes a full COA covering purity, moisture content, and microbial standards.
