FAQ

Common buyer questions

Why is Unjha significant for cumin sourcing?Unjha in North Gujarat is India's largest cumin trading hub — the majority of India's cumin production passes through its APMC market. Sourcing from Unjha means direct access to competitive pricing, wide grade availability and established processing units.
What is the difference between machine cleaned and sortex cleaned cumin?Machine cleaned removes physical impurities like stones and dust. Sortex cleaning uses optical technology to additionally remove discoloured seeds and foreign matter — resulting in higher visual purity preferred by food processors and spice brands.
Can you supply EU MRL-compliant cumin?Yes, product can be supplied with pesticide residue analysis as per EU MRL requirements on request — particularly relevant for European importers and food manufacturers.
Do you offer new crop or old crop cumin?Both new crop and old crop availability are confirmed at enquiry stage depending on season and buyer requirement.
Do you offer pre-shipment samples?Yes, samples can be arranged on request, typically via courier, prior to order confirmation.
Which port do you ship from?Primarily Mundra or Kandla, Gujarat, depending on shipment schedule and destination.
