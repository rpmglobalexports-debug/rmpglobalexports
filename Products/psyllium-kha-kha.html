Psyllium Kha-Kha
Industrial Powder

⚠️ Industrial product only. Kha-Kha is a byproduct of psyllium husk processing. It is not suitable for human consumption, food manufacturing, or pharmaceutical use. If you are looking for food-grade or pharma-grade psyllium, see our Psyllium Husk page.
Industrial Byproduct

What Is Psyllium Kha-Kha?

Kha-Kha is the residual powder generated during the milling and processing of psyllium husk (isabgol) from the seed husks of Plantago ovata. It contains a significant proportion of the seed coat and endosperm material that does not meet the purity or fiber standards required for food or pharmaceutical applications.

Despite not being suitable for human use, Kha-Kha retains the natural mucilaginous (binding) properties of psyllium. When added to water or soil it swells and forms a gel, making it valuable as a natural binder and stabilizer in industrial applications. Gujarat's large-scale psyllium processing industry produces Kha-Kha as a reliable byproduct stream, and RPM Global Exports can supply it alongside food-grade psyllium enquiries.

Industrial Applications

🏗️
Soil Stabilization

Mixed into soil or roadbed material to improve binding and cohesion. Used in civil engineering and road construction as a natural soil amendment.

🌿
Erosion Control

Applied to slopes, embankments, and disturbed land to bind soil and reduce surface runoff. Widely used in revegetation projects and highway erosion control.

💨
Dust Suppression

Natural mucilaginous properties make it effective for dust suppression on unpaved roads, mining sites, and open storage areas when mixed with water and sprayed.

🐄
Animal Feed Binder

Used as a natural binding agent in pelleted animal feed formulations. Improves pellet integrity and cohesion during feed manufacturing. Suitable for ruminant and poultry feed applications.
