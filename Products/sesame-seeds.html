Sesame Seeds

Gujarat Sourcing

Premium Sesame Seeds,
Direct from Gujarat

RPM Global Exports sources sesame seeds from Gujarat's premier growing regions, where warm climates and centuries of cultivation produce seeds with high oil content and exceptional purity. Our supplier relationships — built through regular market visits to Unjha APMC — allow us to maintain consistent grade standards across shipments.

We offer three grades: Hulled (for tahini, hummus, halva, and premium bakery — the hull is removed for a clean white seed), Natural White Sortex-Cleaned (for oil extraction, bakery toppings, and confectionery — hull intact, high oil content), and Black Sesame (for East Asian confectionery, health foods, and specialty bakery). Each grade comes with a spec sheet and sample availability.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions from Importers

What is the minimum order quantity for sesame seeds?
Our minimum order quantity for sesame seeds is 1 MT per shipment. Trial orders below 1 MT are considered case by case — contact us to discuss your requirement.
Can we request a sample before placing a bulk order?
Yes, we provide pre-shipment samples for quality verification. Contact us at meet@rpmglobalexports.com to request one.
What certifications does RPM Global Exports hold?
We are registered with IEC and APEDA, ensuring compliance with Indian export regulations and international quality standards.
What are your payment terms?
We work with T/T (Telegraphic Transfer). Terms are discussed based on order size and buyer profile.
How long does shipment take to the USA or Germany?
Typical transit time is 18–25 days to the USA and 15–20 days to Germany via sea freight.
Do you provide a Certificate of Analysis with each shipment?
Yes, every shipment includes a full COA covering purity, moisture content, and microbial standards.
