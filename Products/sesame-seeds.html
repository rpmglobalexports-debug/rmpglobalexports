FAQ

Common buyer questions

What is the difference between natural and hulled sesame seeds?Natural sesame seeds retain their outer husk and have a slightly stronger flavour — commonly used in bakery and oil extraction. Hulled sesame seeds have the husk removed, giving a milder taste and whiter appearance — preferred for tahini, food manufacturing and retail products.
What is the difference between single and double sortex?Double sortex passes the product through the cleaning machine twice, removing more foreign matter and discoloured seeds — used where buyers require higher visual and purity standards.
Do you offer pre-shipment samples?Yes, samples can be arranged on request, typically via courier, prior to order confirmation.
What documents do you provide with each shipment?Commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin and other documentation as required by the destination country, coordinated per shipment.
What is the minimum order quantity?1 MT or as per buyer requirement. We are happy to discuss trial shipments for new buyers.
Which port do you ship from?Primarily Mundra or Kandla, Gujarat, depending on shipment schedule and destination.
