Bulk Chia Seeds for Supplement & Food Brands

Salvia hispanica · Whole Seeds

Chia Seeds for
Functional Food & Supplement Brands

Chia seeds (Salvia hispanica) are used across dietary supplement, functional food, and consumer packaged goods manufacturing — primarily for their omega-3 fatty acid content, high dietary fibre, and protein profile. They are a standard ingredient in health food products, beverage mixes, granola and snack formulations, and dietary supplement capsule blends.

We supply chia seeds in bulk for manufacturers and distributors. Enquire with your specification — grade, packaging format, quantity, destination port, and any regulatory requirements — and we will confirm current supply details, pricing, and dispatch a pre-shipment sample with third-party CoA for your quality verification.

HSN Code & Customs Classification

Chia seeds are commonly classified under HSN 12079990 in Indian export documentation — covering other oil seeds and oleaginous fruits not elsewhere specified under Chapter 12.

Important: The HSN code shown here is indicative and for reference only. HSN classification is a customs determination — your customs broker and our CHA must confirm the correct code before it appears on any shipping document or commercial invoice. Do not use this code in any formal document without independent verification.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions from Buyers

What form of chia seeds do you supply?
We supply whole chia seeds — the standard bulk commodity form used in food manufacturing, dietary supplements, functional beverages, and consumer packaged goods. Both black and white chia seeds are available; confirm which grade you need at enquiry.
Where are your chia seeds sourced from?
Origin details are confirmed at enquiry stage alongside the pre-shipment sample and CoA. Chia is grown in several regions globally — we confirm exact origin, supply chain, and any country-of-origin documentation requirements at the time of your enquiry.
Can we get a sample before placing a bulk order?
Yes. We dispatch a pre-shipment sample before any bulk commitment — the sample cost is charged but credited toward your order if you place one. Samples come with a third-party lab CoA covering nutrition profile (fat, omega-3, fibre, protein), moisture, and microbial limits.
What nutrition parameters do you certify on CoA?
Standard third-party CoA covers moisture, total fat, omega-3 fatty acids (ALA), dietary fibre, protein, heavy metals, and microbial limits. If you need additional parameters for your market's regulatory or labelling requirements, tell us at enquiry and we will confirm what the lab can test.
Does your APEDA registration cover chia seeds?
Yes — our APEDA RCMC (Reg. RCMC/APEDA/31367/2026-2031) covers psyllium husk, ashwagandha, and chia seeds under the Herbal and Medicinal Plants panel. See our Certifications page for the full registration document.
What is the HSN code for chia seeds?
Chia seeds are commonly classified under HSN 12079990 in Indian export documentation. However, HSN classification is a customs determination — confirm the correct code with your customs broker or our CHA before using it in any shipping document. This code is displayed here for reference only.
