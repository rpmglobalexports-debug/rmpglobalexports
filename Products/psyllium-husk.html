Psyllium Husk

Gujarat Isabgol Belt

Psyllium Husk for
Global Health Markets

India produces over 80% of the world's psyllium (isabgol), and Gujarat — particularly the regions of Mehsana, Banaskantha, and Patan — sits at the heart of that supply. RPM Global Exports sources psyllium husk directly from Gujarat's established isabgol processing units, with NABL-accredited lab CoA available on request.

We supply three distinct psyllium products: Whole Husk Food Grade (for food manufacturers and supplement brands), Whole Husk Pharma Grade (for regulated markets — US, Canada, EU), and Husk Powder (for capsule, sachet, and drink mix applications). Industrial Kha-Kha — a processing byproduct used in soil stabilization and construction — is on a separate page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions from Importers

What purity grade and mesh size do you supply for psyllium husk?
We supply psyllium husk at 99% purity, 40 mesh (standard). Finer mesh grades available on request. Every batch includes a third-party NABL lab CoA confirming purity, moisture, and microbial counts.
What is the difference between food-grade and pharma-grade psyllium husk?
Food-grade meets FSSAI dietary fibre standards. Pharma-grade meets stricter IP/USP microbial and heavy metal limits. We supply both — specify your end-use when enquiring so we can confirm the right spec.
What is the MOQ, and can I trial before committing to bulk?
MOQ is 1 MT. First-time buyers can trial from 500 kg. A free pre-shipment sample is available before you commit to any quantity — no payment required to receive the sample.
Does your APEDA registration cover psyllium husk?
Yes. We hold APEDA RCMC specifically for psyllium husk (Reg. RCMC/APEDA/31367/2026–2031, valid to June 2031, Herbal & Medicinal Plants panel). This is the relevant export authority for this product.
What packaging is available for psyllium husk?
Standard is 25 kg HDPE bags with inner poly liner. 50 kg bags and custom private-label packaging available on request. We share packing photos and video on WhatsApp before dispatch.
