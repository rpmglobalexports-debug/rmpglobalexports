Psyllium Husk

Gujarat Isabgol Belt

Psyllium Husk for
Global Health Markets

India produces over 80% of the world's psyllium (isabgol), and Gujarat — particularly the regions of Mehsana, Banaskantha, and Patan — sits at the heart of that supply. RPM Global Exports sources psyllium husk from Gujarat's established isabgol processing units, ensuring consistent quality that meets both food-grade and pharmaceutical-grade specifications.

Our psyllium husk is available in food-grade and pharma-grade variants. Pharma-grade is suitable for regulated markets including the US, Canada, and the EU, with higher swelling factor standards and stricter ash and contamination limits. All batches come with spec sheets and are available for sample order. We offer flexible packaging in 25 kg multiwall paper bags or PP bags.
