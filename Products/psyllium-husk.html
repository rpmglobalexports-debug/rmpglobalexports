FAQ

Common buyer questions

What is ispaghula husk — is it the same as psyllium husk?Yes. Ispaghula husk is the name used in UK, European and pharmaceutical markets for the same product known as psyllium husk in North America and other regions. Both refer to the husk of Plantago ovata seeds, sourced primarily from Gujarat, India.
What is the swelling factor of your psyllium husk grades?Minimum 40 ml/g for the 85% grade, with higher values for 95% and 98% grades — confirmed per batch on request.
Do you supply pharma-grade psyllium? What certifications?Yes, BP/EP/USP grade compliance is available on request, along with COA covering microbial limits and heavy metal testing.
Do you offer pre-shipment samples?Yes, samples can be arranged on request, typically via courier, prior to order confirmation.
What is the minimum order quantity?1 MT or as per buyer requirement. We are happy to discuss trial shipments for new buyers.
Which port do you ship from?Primarily Mundra or Kandla, Gujarat, depending on shipment schedule and destination.
