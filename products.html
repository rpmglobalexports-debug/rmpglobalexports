why work with us

we're middlemen.
but the good kind.

most indian psyllium sellers push one processor's output and hope the spec fits. we pick the processor per spec, take the invoice ourselves, and sort out what india is bad at before it reaches you.

01

we absorb the working-capital

indian processors want 50% advance on large orders. we invoice you as a merchant exporter, so your pos look like any other supplier payment, not a wire-first prepayment to a factory you've never been to.

02

processor fit per spec

a lead target under 0.08 mg/kg sends you to a different processor than a kosher-certified powder for a premium brand. we pick, you don't have to vet five factories.

03

500 kg trials are fine

most distributors will quote you a full container at 18 mt minimum. we start at 500 kg so qualification doesn't cost a quarter of inventory. scale when you're ready.

04

compliance pack ready, not promised

fssc, nabl lot reports, gmp cert, declaration pack, process flow, factory video, 360 tour. sent as one pdf when you ask, not emailed back three weeks later after your qa chases.

05

24-hour response, from one person

you'll be dealing with meet, co-founder. not a sales channel. not a cc list. your questions get an answer in a day, or an honest note that it'll take longer and why.

06

10 to 14 day lead time

from order confirmation, from unjha to fob mundra. we hold the processor accountable on dates so you can plan around a real number, not a hopeful one.
