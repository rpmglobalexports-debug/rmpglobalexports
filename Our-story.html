Who We Are

Built on Direct Relationships
and Honest Pricing

Two founders from Vadodara, Gujarat. One Unjha sourcing hub. A shared mission to make Indian agri exports straightforward for serious buyers worldwide.

RPM Global Exports is a founder-run agricultural export business based in Vadodara, Gujarat, India. We export sesame seeds, cumin seeds, and psyllium husk to importers in the United States, Canada, and Germany — handling everything from sourcing to documentation directly, without trading house intermediaries.

The Beginning

Two Brothers, One Mission

Rudra Jani and Meet Jani grew up in Vadodara, Gujarat — a region with deep roots in agricultural trade. Watching Gujarat's agricultural wealth flow through intermediary chains, they saw an opportunity: connect Gujarat's best growers and market hubs directly to international buyers who needed reliable, traceable supply.

Sourcing

Rudra: Building at the Source

Rudra manages all sourcing and procurement for RPM Global Exports. His work takes him regularly to Unjha APMC — Asia's largest seed and spice trading market — where he builds and maintains relationships with established commission agents, farmers, and processing units. By being present at the market, Rudra tracks quality grade variations, crop cycles, and price movements in real time. When a buyer sends us a requirement, the quotation we return reflects what's actually available at actual market prices.

Market Understanding

Meet: Understanding the Buyer's World

Meet Jani spent time in Canada, which gave him direct exposure to how importers and distributors think in Western markets — what documentation they need, what quality standards matter, what questions come up before a first shipment. That perspective informs how RPM Global Exports communicates with buyers: clearly, practically, and without the vague language that plagues many export enquiries. Meet handles all buyer relations and directly manages every incoming enquiry.

Why Founder-Led Matters

No Middlemen in the Conversation

Many Indian exporters route buyer communication through sales staff who weren't in the market last week and haven't seen this season's crops. At RPM, when you email or WhatsApp us, Rudra or Meet responds — not a junior executive reading from a template. This means faster, more accurate responses, and quotations that you can actually rely on.

Where We're Headed

Building a Trusted Name in Agri Exports

RPM Global Exports is still a young company, and we're deliberate about that. We'd rather build deep, repeat relationships with a small number of serious importers than push volume through dozens of one-time buyers. If you're looking for a long-term sourcing partner for sesame, cumin, or psyllium husk — one that will be responsive, honest about quality, and reliable on documentation — we're building for exactly that.
