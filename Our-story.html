Our Story

Built to make Indian sourcing clearer, faster and more reliable.

RPM Global Exports was started with a simple belief: global buyers should be able to source Indian agricultural products with clear communication, practical product details and dependable follow-up.

We are based in Gujarat, India, one of the strong sourcing regions for spices, seeds and agri products. Our current focus is on sesame seeds, cumin seeds and psyllium husk, while additional products are handled only when buyer requirements are clear.

Why we started RPM Global Exports

Many importers want to buy from India, but the process can feel unclear from outside. Product specifications, supplier communication, documentation, packaging options and follow-ups often become complicated when there is no single responsive point of contact.

We started RPM Global Exports to bridge that gap. Our goal is not to list hundreds of products. Instead, we focus on selected core products, understand buyer requirements properly and coordinate with supplier networks to support practical sourcing from India.

For a new buyer, trust matters. That is why we are building RPM with real founder-led communication, product clarity, supplier visits, documentation support and transparent follow-up from enquiry to shipment.

Our background

RPM Global Exports is built by a young founder team with complementary responsibilities. One side focuses on supplier coordination, sourcing and documentation support. The other side focuses on buyer communication, international outreach and business development.

This structure helps us respond faster, understand both supplier and buyer expectations, and keep the process organized. We are actively building relationships with suppliers, visiting markets, improving product documentation and creating buyer-ready export material.

What we are building

We are building RPM Global Exports as a focused Indian agri export company for importers, wholesalers, distributors and ingredient buyers. Our priority is to become a reliable sourcing partner for buyers who need consistent communication and practical product information from India.

As we grow, we are improving our product specification sheets, supplier network, quality coordination process, packaging options and export documentation support. We believe long-term export business is built through trust, consistency and follow-up — not only through one-time quotations.
