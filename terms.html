General terms of engagement for trade enquiries, quotations, samples, and export orders with RPM Global Exports.

Last updated: 14 August 2026

These terms govern the relationship between RPM Global Exports ("RPM", "we", "us") and buyers, importers, or other parties ("you") who engage with us through this website, email, or WhatsApp. By submitting an enquiry or placing an order, you accept these terms.

⚖️ Legal review recommended Several clauses in this document — particularly Sections 8 (Liability), 9 (Dispute Resolution), and 10 (Governing Law) — contain placeholder language that should be reviewed by a qualified legal professional before this document is treated as binding. RPM should not rely on these terms in a dispute without first having them vetted.

1. Nature of Enquiries

Submitting an enquiry through our website, email, or WhatsApp does not constitute a binding order or contract. It is an invitation to trade — we will review your requirement and respond with relevant information, pricing, or a quotation.

No obligation arises on either side until a formal Purchase Order (PO) is issued by you and accepted in writing by RPM Global Exports.

2. Quotations

All quotations issued by RPM Global Exports are:

  • Based on live Unjha APMC market rates at the time of quotation
  • Valid for the period stated in the quotation (typically 24–72 hours) due to commodity price fluctuations
  • Subject to reconfirmation at PO stage — if market conditions have changed materially between the quotation date and PO acceptance, we will notify you and provide an updated figure before proceeding
  • Based on the specifications stated in the quotation (purity, grade, packaging, Incoterms, destination port) — changing any parameter may alter the price

A quotation is not a binding commitment to supply at the quoted price beyond its stated validity window.

3. Minimum Order Quantities

  • Trial orders: 500 kg — available for first-time buyers to verify quality before committing to volume
  • Standard MOQ: 1 metric ton (MT) for ongoing wholesale/bulk pricing
  • Full container load (FCL) quantities are quoted at separately negotiated rates

We may accept quantities below these thresholds at our discretion. If your requirement is below the standard MOQ, contact us directly to discuss.

4. Samples

Sample requests are handled on a case-by-case basis. Where a sample is provided:

  • It will be accompanied by a third-party Certificate of Analysis (CoA) from a NABL-accredited or internationally recognised laboratory
  • The sample represents the available stock grade and packing at the time of dispatch — it is not a guarantee that all future shipments will be identical, though we aim for batch consistency
  • Sample costs and shipping charges may apply depending on destination and quantity

5. Payment Terms

Payment terms are agreed case-by-case and stated explicitly in the Proforma Invoice (PI) or Sales Contract. We do not publish fixed payment terms on this website as they depend on the buyer's history, order value, and destination country.

Common structures include advance payment (T/T) and Letter of Credit (L/C), but these are not default terms — they are negotiated per transaction.

⚖️ Legal review recommended If you intend to use standardised payment terms (e.g. specific L/C conditions, advance percentage requirements, credit terms for repeat buyers), these should be documented in a separate Sales Contract template reviewed by legal counsel rather than relying on this general terms document.

6. Specifications and Quality

All shipments are made against the specifications stated in the Proforma Invoice or Sales Contract (purity %, swelling index, moisture, mesh size, packaging). If the shipped material does not meet the agreed specification, you should:

  • Notify us in writing within 7 days of receiving the shipment
  • Provide a third-party CoA from an accredited lab confirming the deviation

We will investigate and respond within 10 business days. Remedies may include replacement, credit, or price adjustment — to be agreed case-by-case.

Claims based solely on in-house buyer testing without independent lab verification will not be accepted as the basis for a quality dispute.

7. Shipping and Risk

Incoterms applicable to each shipment are stated in the Proforma Invoice. Unless otherwise agreed:

  • Under FOB terms, risk transfers to the buyer when goods pass the ship's rail at the named port of loading
  • Under CIF/CFR terms, we arrange freight and (under CIF) insurance, but risk transfers at the same point

We are not responsible for delays caused by port congestion, shipping line scheduling, customs holds at destination, or events outside our control after the goods have been handed to the carrier.
