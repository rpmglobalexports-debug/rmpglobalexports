About Indian Sesame Seeds

Sesame seeds are one of India's important agricultural export products. They are used across food processing, bakery, confectionery, spice blends, tahini manufacturing, edible oil extraction, snack production, and retail food packaging. For importers, sesame seeds are not only a commodity purchase. The right supplier must understand purity, moisture, seed color, admixture, sorting, packaging, documentation, and shipment planning.

Buyers searching for an Indian sesame seeds exporter usually want three things: reliable quality, competitive pricing, and smooth export execution. A low price alone is not enough if the shipment has inconsistent quality, weak communication, poor documentation, or packaging that does not match the buyer's destination market. RPM Global Exports focuses on helping buyers source sesame seeds with clear specifications and practical export support.

Why Source Sesame Seeds from India?

India has a long history of sesame cultivation and trade. Gujarat is one of the important sourcing regions connected with oilseeds, spices, and agricultural commodities. The region has access to farmers, traders, cleaning units, processors, packers, transporters, and western Indian ports such as Mundra and Kandla. This makes Gujarat a practical base for sourcing sesame seeds for export.

International buyers often source Indian sesame seeds because of the available variety, established trade network, flexible packaging, and experience with food export documentation. Depending on the buyer's requirement, sesame seeds can be supplied for bakery use, tahini, oil extraction, spice blends, retail packs, wholesale distribution, or further processing.

Types of Sesame Seeds Buyers Commonly Source

Natural Sesame Seeds

Natural sesame seeds retain their outer hull and are commonly used in food processing, bakery toppings, spice blends, oil extraction, and wholesale trading. Buyers usually evaluate natural sesame seeds by color, purity, moisture, foreign matter, admixture, and overall cleanliness.

Hulled Sesame Seeds

Hulled sesame seeds have the outer hull removed and are widely used in bakery, confectionery, tahini, ready-to-eat food, and premium food applications. Hulled sesame normally requires more processing than natural sesame, so buyers should clearly mention required purity, color, microbial limits, and intended use.

Sortex Cleaned Sesame Seeds

Sortex cleaned sesame seeds are processed through color sorting and cleaning systems to reduce impurities, discolored seeds, foreign matter, and uneven particles. Buyers requiring export-grade sesame usually ask for sortex cleaned material because consistency matters in food manufacturing.

Quality Parameters Importers Should Check

Sesame quality should be discussed before price negotiation. Important parameters include purity, moisture, foreign matter, admixture, seed color, size consistency, broken seeds, immature seeds, odor, infestation, and processing level. For food buyers, microbial limits, pesticide residue requirements, and country-specific compliance may also matter.

Importers should share the exact intended use before asking for a quotation. Sesame for tahini, bakery topping, oil extraction, spice blending, and retail packing may require different grades. When the buyer shares grade, quantity, packaging, destination port, and target shipment timeline, the exporter can give a more practical quotation.

Export Packaging Options

Sesame seeds must be packed in clean, dry, and export-suitable packaging. Common bulk packaging options include 25 kg and 50 kg bags. Depending on the buyer's requirement and supplier capacity, sesame can be packed in PP bags, paper bags, laminated bags, or customized branded packing. For retail or private-label buyers, additional packing discussions may be required.

Packaging must be selected according to destination, storage condition, handling method, and container movement. Moisture protection is important because sesame seeds can lose quality if exposed to unsuitable storage or transit conditions. Buyers should confirm whether they need palletization, bag marking, batch details, labeling support, or specific packaging material.

How to Choose the Right Sesame Seeds Exporter from India

A reliable sesame exporter should be able to answer practical questions clearly. What grade is available? Is the sesame natural or hulled? Is it machine cleaned or sortex cleaned? What is the expected moisture level? Which packing is available? Which port will be used? Can the supplier support documents requested by the buyer? These questions are more useful than asking only for the lowest price.

Before confirming an order, buyers should request specifications, samples where practical, packaging details, payment terms, delivery timeline, and documentation scope. This reduces misunderstanding and helps both buyer and exporter move faster from inquiry to shipment.
