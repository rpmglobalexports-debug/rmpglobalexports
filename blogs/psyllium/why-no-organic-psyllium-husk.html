Psyllium Husk / Organic Supply

Why Is There No Organic Psyllium Husk Available Right Now?

As a merchant exporter, RPM Global Exports works with a wide network of manufacturers and processors across Gujarat's psyllium belt. When buyers started asking us for organic psyllium husk this season, we didn't take one processor's "not available" at face value. We checked across our entire network — and got the same answer everywhere.

Send Requirement View Psyllium Husk

The answer came back the same everywhere: the organic crop exists. What doesn't exist right now, according to what every processor in our network is telling us, is the paperwork that lets it be sold as organic.

The crop is clean. The certificate isn't there — at least, that's the word across the market.

The TC Gap: What's Actually Missing

The consistent talk we're hearing from processors and certifiers across our network is that Transaction Certificates are not currently being issued against this season's already-harvested organic psyllium crop. We haven't seen an official APEDA notice spelling this out in those exact terms — this is market chatter, not a claim we're making ourselves — but it's the same story from every source we've checked, and it lines up with the broader certification-body crackdown APEDA has been running.

The psyllium Transaction Certificate is the document that's supposed to carry organic status through the supply chain — from farm to processor to exporter to the label on a D2C brand's package. If that document genuinely isn't being issued right now, a lot can be pesticide-free, tested clean, and functionally identical to certified organic husk, and still not be legally sellable as organic. The gap wouldn't be in the crop. It would be in the paper trail.

If what we're hearing holds, this isn't a quality problem or a supply-volume problem — it's a certification-timing problem. The market talk points to the organic psyllium 2027 crop cycle as when TCs against fresh organic harvest are expected to resume. We'd treat that as a working estimate, not a confirmed date, until we see it play out.

Who This Affects

US and Canadian Buyers

From what we're seeing and hearing, this bottleneck is hitting US and Canadian buyers specifically. Organic claims into the US market require psyllium NOP certification recognized under USDA NOP, and Canada has its own equivalent requirement — both of which would run through this same TC chain that processors say is currently interrupted.

If you're sourcing organic psyllium husk for the USA or Canada and a supplier is currently claiming certified organic stock with a valid Transaction Certificate against this season's lot, ask to see it before you move forward. Our psyllium husk supplier verification guide covers what documents to request and how to check whether they're legitimate.

EU Buyers

Europe looks different. India's NPOP standard has an EU equivalence arrangement, and that recognition pathway isn't part of what's being described as affected. If your organic psyllium husk is destined for the EU, supply through NPOP-certified channels appears to be continuing without the same interruption — though as always, confirm current certification status on any specific lot before you commit.

What We're Telling Buyers Right Now

USA / Canada buyers: if organic certification is a hard requirement for your label, treat the 2027 crop as the working timeline, based on what the market is saying — and verify it independently before you plan around it. In the meantime, we can supply conventional psyllium husk from the same clean, well-tested lots — same purity grades, same CoA rigor — for any use case where the organic label isn't the deciding factor.

EU buyers: organic supply through NPOP-equivalent channels appears to be available now. Get in touch and we'll confirm current certification status, grade, and lead time against your specific programme before anything is booked.

Every buyer: ask any supplier claiming organic psyllium for the US or Canada right now for the actual current Transaction Certificate — not last season's, not a certifier's general accreditation. If they can't produce it against this season's lot, the "organic" claim isn't backed by anything that will hold up on your end.

This is what we're hearing and seeing across our network, not an official ruling we're citing — but it's consistent enough, from enough sources, that we think buyers should know before they plan around organic supply that may not be there. If you want to be kept in the loop as the 2027 crop moves through certification, or you want conventional supply in the meantime, reach out and we'll walk you through exactly where things stand.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Why is there no organic psyllium husk available for US and Canada buyers?

Based on what processors and certifiers across our Gujarat network are reporting, Transaction Certificates are not currently being issued against this season's already-harvested organic psyllium crop. The crop itself exists and tests clean — but without a valid TC for this season's lot, it cannot legally be sold as organic in the US or Canadian market. This is market information from our network, not an official APEDA ruling.

2. What is a Transaction Certificate for organic psyllium husk?

A Transaction Certificate (TC) is issued by an organic certification body for each transaction in the supply chain — farm to processor, processor to exporter, exporter to buyer. For psyllium husk destined for the US under USDA NOP requirements, each shipment needs a valid TC against the current season's crop lot. Without it, the organic claim has no verifiable backing in the destination market.

3. Are EU buyers affected by the organic psyllium husk TC issue?

Based on what we're hearing, no. India's NPOP standard has an equivalence arrangement with the EU, and that pathway is not being described as part of the current interruption. NPOP-certified organic psyllium husk for EU-destined supply appears to be available. Confirm the certification status on any specific lot before committing.

4. When will certified organic psyllium husk be available again?

The market talk we're hearing points to the 2027 crop cycle as when TCs against fresh organic psyllium harvest are expected to resume. Treat this as a working estimate — not a confirmed date — and verify it independently before building supply plans around it.

5. What can US/Canada buyers source in the meantime?

RPM Global Exports supplies conventional psyllium husk — food grade and pharma grade — from clean, well-tested lots with third-party NABL-accredited CoA on every shipment. For buyers where the organic label isn't a hard requirement, conventional supply is available now. Trial orders from 500 kg; sample before you commit — cost credited toward your order.*
