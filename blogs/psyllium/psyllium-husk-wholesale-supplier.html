If you're looking for a psyllium husk wholesale supplier that can deliver consistent quality, fair pricing, and a smooth export process, India is the obvious starting point — it grows and processes the majority of the world's psyllium husk, and almost every major brand you've heard of sources from here in some form.

At RPM Global Exports, we source directly from Unjha, Gujarat — the center of India's psyllium trade and home to the country's largest psyllium husk market (APMC). Working close to the source means shorter supply chains, better visibility into processing, and pricing that isn't padded by multiple middlemen.

Why Source From Unjha, Gujarat

Most psyllium husk traded globally passes through Unjha at some stage, whether buyers know it or not. Sourcing directly from this region — rather than through a trader several steps removed from it — gives you:

  • Closer visibility into how the husk is cleaned, sieved, and graded
  • Faster response on availability and pricing, since we're not waiting on a third party
  • The ability to inspect or request samples tied to a specific processing batch
  • Pricing that reflects actual mandi rates, not layered markups

We're IEC and APEDA registered (IEC: DFLPJ8868G), which means we're authorized and audited for legal agricultural export from India — not just a trading name on a website.

Quality You Can Actually Verify

Every shipment is checked against the buyer's agreed specification before dispatch — purity, moisture content, color, and cleanliness are the variables that matter most for psyllium husk, and we expect buyers to ask about all four. If you have a target purity percentage or swell factor in mind, send it over; we'll confirm whether we can meet it before quoting, not after.

Export Documentation
