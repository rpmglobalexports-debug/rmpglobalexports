Privacy Policy

How RPM Global Exports collects, uses, and protects information submitted through this website.

Last updated: 14 August 2026

RPM Global Exports ("we", "us", "our") is a merchant export business registered in Vadodara, Gujarat, India. This policy explains what information we collect when you use rpmglobalexports.com, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information only when you voluntarily submit it through our website. The trade enquiry form (on the homepage and contact page) collects:

  • Full name — required
  • Email address — required
  • Product interest — required (Psyllium Husk, Cumin Seeds, Sesame Seeds, or Other)
  • Quantity (MT) — required
  • Your requirement — required (grade, port, packaging preferences, etc.)
  • Company name — optional
  • WhatsApp number — optional
  • Country — optional
  • Destination port — optional

The spec sheet request form collects:

  • Email address — to send product specification sheets

We do not install cookies beyond those set automatically by Google Analytics (see Section 4). We do not collect payment information, passport/ID details, or any sensitive personal data.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through our enquiry form is used solely to:

  • Respond to your trade enquiry with a quotation or relevant information
  • Follow up on ongoing sourcing discussions
  • Send spec sheets you have specifically requested

We do not use your information for unsolicited marketing, automated profiling, or any purpose unrelated to your enquiry.

3. Who Receives Your Data

Enquiry submissions are processed through Formspree (formspree.io), a third-party form handling service, and delivered directly to our inbox. Formspree's own privacy policy governs how they handle form data in transit — you can review it at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any other third parties for commercial purposes. We may share information if required by law or a legitimate legal process.

4. Google Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors find and use our pages (page views, referral sources, approximate geographic location). Google Analytics uses cookies and collects anonymised data. It does not collect the information you submit through our forms.

To opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites, you can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

5. Data Retention

We retain enquiry information for as long as needed to manage the business relationship or potential trade transaction. If you would like us to delete your information, contact us at meet@rpmglobalexports.com and we will do so promptly.

6. Security

Our website is served over HTTPS. Form submissions are transmitted through Formspree's encrypted infrastructure. As a small business, we do not operate a formal information security programme, but we do not store your form data on our own servers — it is delivered to our email inbox only.

7. Your Rights

You may request at any time:

  • Access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion of your information

To exercise any of these rights, email meet@rpmglobalexports.com. We will respond within 10 business days.

8. Children

This website is intended for business-to-business trade enquiries. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18 years of age.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the business grows or if our practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any revisions. Continued use of this website after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
