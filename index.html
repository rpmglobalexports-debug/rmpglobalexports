Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions from Importers

What is your minimum order quantity?
First-time buyers can start with a 500 kg trial order. Our standard MOQ after that is 1 MT. We built our model so you can prove us on a small order before scaling.
Can we request a sample before placing a bulk order?
Yes — and the sample is free. Request directly on WhatsApp or email meet@rpmglobalexports.com. Samples come with a third-party lab CoA so you have independently verified quality data before committing to bulk.
We've never worked with an Indian supplier before — how do we reduce risk?

We designed our process around exactly this concern. Here's how first-time buyers typically work with us:

  1. Free sample first — we ship a sample to you before any payment. Test it yourself or send it to your lab.
  2. Third-party lab CoA — independently verified certificate of analysis, not in-house claims.
  3. Photos and video of your lot — before dispatch, we send visual confirmation of your actual material.
  4. Start at 500 kg — trial at a fraction of the commitment most exporters require.
  5. Third-party inspection welcome — appoint your own agency; we work openly with them.
  6. Flexible first-order payment — discussed case by case based on your profile and order size.

WhatsApp us directly — the founders respond personally.

What certifications does RPM Global Exports hold?
We hold an active IEC (Import Export Code), APEDA RCMC covering psyllium husk (Reg. No. RCMC/APEDA/31367/2026-2027), GST registration, and MSME/Udyam registration. Spice Board registration (cumin) and IOPEPC registration (sesame) are in progress. See our Certifications page for full details.
What are your payment terms?
We work with T/T (Telegraphic Transfer). Terms are discussed based on order size and buyer profile.
How long does shipment take to the USA or Germany?
Typical transit time is 18–25 days to the USA and 15–20 days to Germany via sea freight.
Do you provide a Certificate of Analysis with each shipment?
Yes, every shipment includes a full COA covering purity, moisture content, and microbial standards.
